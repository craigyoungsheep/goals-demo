Aurora: a smaller permissions release

Decision record · Design, Engineering and Support

Options considered

The author brought two interaction models: predefined role templates, and fully customisable roles. They mapped risky edge cases before the scope discussion. Maya owned the authorisation design.

Decision & rationale

The team agreed role templates first and custom roles later. The research showed administrators struggled to understand existing role names, giving the team a concrete comprehension problem to test before adding more configuration.

What partners added

Support contributed four previously unaccounted-for cases: an invitation resent after a role changed; a contractor with no billing access; a role removed while an invite is pending; and an invitation for an existing member. These became acceptance criteria.

Ownership & unresolved work

Maya owns the engineering plan. The author owns testing. Daniel owns the keyboard-focus fix. Pilot expansion remains on hold pending retest; no delivery-date saving has been measured.

All people, projects and results on this page are fictional. This is a source document for the curated demo, not a verified workplace record.
