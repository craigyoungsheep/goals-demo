Aurora: research & pilot readout

From confusing role names to clearer access decisions

The question

Can an administrator invite a contractor without giving them billing access? In eight research sessions, five administrators could not explain the access implied by the role names. The author ran the sessions; Lena recruited the participants.

What changed

The author changed the prototype to describe what each person could do. Maya and Engineering built the pilot release. The author designed and tested the role summaries.

What we observed

In the new pilot, 24 of 30 administrators completed the invite with the correct access without help. The previous pilot recorded 15 of 30 on the same task definition. These were separate cohorts, not a randomised comparison: this is a directional signal, not a causal estimate.

Limits & release gate

Retention, revenue and time saved have not been measured. A keyboard-focus defect remains open with Daniel. Expansion is on hold until the fix is retested.
